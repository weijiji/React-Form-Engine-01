# Product Specification Generator

## Purpose

Transform Requirement Specification into an implementation-neutral Product Specification.

Do not jump into technology choices.

The goal is to define system behavior clearly enough that developers, QA and AI agents share the same understanding.

## Constraints

Do NOT specify:

- Programming language
- Framework
- Database
- Architecture

Those belong to Design Specification.
